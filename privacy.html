Kash OS

Privacy

Last updated 11 August 2026

Kash OS is a personal, single-user Health OS. It uses the official WHOOP API only after the account owner grants access through WHOOP OAuth.

Data used

The app may retrieve WHOOP profile, body-measurement, cycle, recovery, sleep, and workout summaries. It also stores training-plan progress and measurements entered by the owner.

Storage and sharing

Sensitive records and integration tokens are encrypted before storage in Postgres. Kash OS does not sell data, serve advertising, or share health data with data brokers. Optional Strava exports occur only when enabled by the owner.

Data minimization

The web service does not store raw sensor frames, ECG streams, precise GPS routes, or continuous high-frequency biometrics. Imported workout records are summary-level.

Control and deletion

The owner can disconnect WHOOP to revoke the OAuth grant. Stored records can be exported or deleted separately. WHOOP data remains subject to WHOOP’s own privacy terms.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to the contact address registered with the Kash OS application in the WHOOP Developer Portal.
